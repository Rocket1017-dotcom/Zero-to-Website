How I Am Building a Website Using 100% AI Ideas

Let's be completely honest: I don't code as a hobby, and none of the design layouts on this page came from my own head. I started this project for one clear reason—to learn a high-income skill and make more money.

From a Typing Test to a Code Editor

It started with a simple curiosity about my laptop keyboard. I realized I never used the function keys, the square brackets, or the curly braces. To figure out what my computer could actually do, I started a course on Typing.com.

The Form Filler's Secret: Breaking the Reliance on the Mouse

As I pushed deeper into my typing course, I didn't just learn where the letters were—I stumbled onto a massive workflow secret that saves hours of digital frustration. I learned that reaching for your mouse is often a trap that slows you down.

Two Keyboard Shortcuts That Changed Everything

1. The Application Speed-Run (The Tab Key)

When filling out long forms or job applications online, I used to manually click into every single box. Now, I hit the Tab key to instantly jump to the next field. If I accidentally skip a box, I hold Shift + Tab to move backward up the page. It completely removes the clunky mouse work.

2. The Instant Habit Wipe (The F5 Key)

During my speed and accuracy tests, I developed an optimization mindset. If I make an error that ruins my 100% accuracy run, I don't waste time finishing a compromised attempt. I hammer F5 to instantly wipe the slate clean and start fresh.
