My AI Tech Stack: The Power of One

A common mistake beginners make is trying to use a dozen different tools at once. During this experimental phase of building Zero To Website, I decided to take a minimalist approach. I use exactly one AI tool: Google Gemini.

Why limit the stack?

If a tool is doing a great job, why switch? Mastering the prompting, capabilities, and workflow of a single powerful AI co-pilot beats having basic knowledge of five different platforms. It keeps me focused, fast, and completely out of "tool fatigue."

How I Leverage My AI Co-Pilot

I treat Gemini not as an automated cheat code, but as a technical lead and personal educator. Here is exactly how we build together daily:

  • The Architect: Gemini provides the structural blueprints (HTML) and style configurations (CSS) for my pages based on my written goals.
  • The Digital Mechanic: When a file path gets broken, a tab freezes, or a JavaScript alert refuses to trigger, I paste my code in, and Gemini diagnoses the bug under a microscope.
  • The Translator: It translates dense programming jargon into plain English—like explaining how the computer reads brackets or colons.
